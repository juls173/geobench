Reading this chart

  • Scores compare only within one image set. GeoBench scales the GeoGuessr formula 5000 × 0.99866017^(m/scale) by the diagonal of the map’s bounds, so a point on ACW and a point on Photospheres are not the same achievement. Switching image sets rebuilds the whole chart.
  • One point per released model, at its best setting. Where a model was run at several reasoning efforts, the point is the highest of them on whichever axis is showing — so the two axes can pick different runs. Tick Every reasoning-effort run to see the full spread.
  • Release dates are Epoch’s, not GeoBench’s. They come from model_metadata.csv, resolved by exact model version where Epoch’s own GeoBench sheet names one. Two models Epoch doesn’t list are dated from their vendor announcements and labelled as such in the tooltip.
  • Refusals score zero. A response GeoBench can’t parse into country / lat / lng counts as a miss and as country-incorrect, which is why a few models sit below where their geolocation ability alone would put them.
