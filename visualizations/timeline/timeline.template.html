Reading this chart

  • Scores compare only within one image set. GeoBench scales the GeoGuessr formula 5000 × 0.99866017^(m/scale) by the diagonal of the map’s bounds, so a point on ACW and a point on Photospheres are not the same achievement. Switching image sets rebuilds the whole chart.
  • One point per released model, at its best setting. Where a model was run at several reasoning efforts, the point is the highest of them on whichever axis is showing — so the two axes can pick different runs. Tick Every reasoning-effort run to see the full spread. Runs that got help the leaderboard’s default setup doesn’t — web search, or a prompt tuned for geolocation — are labelled in the tooltip and can be switched off, since including them compares a tuned setup against everyone else’s standard one.
  • Release dates are Epoch’s, not GeoBench’s. They come from model_metadata.csv, resolved by exact model version where Epoch’s own GeoBench sheet names one. Two models Epoch doesn’t list are dated from their vendor announcements and labelled as such in the tooltip.
  • Refusals score zero. A response GeoBench can’t parse into country / lat / lng counts as a miss and as country-incorrect, which is why a few models sit below where their geolocation ability alone would put them.
  • Why the trend isn’t a straight line. Score and country accuracy are bounded, so a straight line eventually predicts the impossible — fitted linearly, Anthropic’s ACW score reaches a flawless 5,000 by August 2027. Saturating instead fits a line in logit space and bends it back, which is the shape Epoch’s own ECI model assumes (performance = sigmoid(discriminability × (capability − difficulty))). On this data the two fit about equally well — R² differs by ~0.03 — because no lab has yet spent long enough near the ceiling to reveal the bend. They disagree about the future, not the past. Switch between them and watch the right-hand end.
  • Saturation is partly the scoring formula’s doing. Score decays exponentially in distance, so equal gains in accuracy buy ever-smaller gains in points. Median miss is the same runs read as kilometres instead, and it is still falling by a roughly constant factor each year — on ACW the field’s median error more than halves every two years, and Anthropic’s halves in under one. A benchmark that looks close to saturated on points is not yet close to saturated on the thing it measures.
  • The trends include each lab’s whole line-up. Small and cheap models (Haiku, Flash-Lite, GPT-4o mini, o4-mini) sit in the same fit as flagships, so a lab that shipped several small models in a row trends flat. Each card reports n and R² for exactly this reason: OpenAI’s ACW score trend has R² ≈ 0.00, which means no trend, not a flat one. Curves are drawn only across the dates each lab has actually shipped in — nothing is extrapolated.
